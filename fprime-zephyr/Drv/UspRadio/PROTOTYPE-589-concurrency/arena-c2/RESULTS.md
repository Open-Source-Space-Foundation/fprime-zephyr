## Side findings

- **`run` starves.** The async `run` port is priority 0; every internal port is 10. Under continuous TX the 1 Hz tick waited **6.25 s** (opt1) / 33 ms (opt2, start-then-complete TX) in the saturated G run; at a 20 Hz tick the opt1 queue (20 deep) overflowed, which is an `FW_ASSERT` on target. Independent of the option; fix the port priority or bound bursts.
- **RAC abort corner (opt 1/3).** Once per 4 s stress run `stopRadio()` hit its 1 s deadline with the hook in `ABORTED` (state 3): `rp_task_abort` on a not-yet-running task sets `ABORTED` and only runs the arbiter if no radio IRQ is pending; the LOCK path of `rp_callback` returns without `rp_task_call_aborted`. Not seen in opt2 (its `pump()` after the abort keeps running passes until the flag clears). Sample is small.
- **Sketch defects fixed to make opt2 run** (marked `SKETCH:` in `sim/opt2/RalSessionSt.cpp`): `rp_disable_failsafe` arity; `m_state` must be set before `smtc_rac_lock_radio_access` because the pre-callback runs inside it and can unlock on failure.
- **SPI clock.** `spi-max-frequency = <125000>` on v5e makes SPI the dominant cost everywhere (a full harvest is 72 % of a P5 airtime). Any option benefits more from raising it than from the concurrency model; the `RalSessionImpl.cpp` comment "~0.1 ms at 4 MHz" assumes a clock the board does not use.

- Priority inversion on opt3's mutex beyond the source note.
- RadioHead-compat framing, CW on the air (CW path exercised only in `initfail`).
